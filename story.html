FROM REDDIT TO KORE

KORE. started from two Reddit users, that saw potential and a lot of creativity in the posts. Users would drop their thoughts, with a lot of pain points mentionned, a really interesting opportunity to help the community is born. They started to develop the tools, and KORE. was born, 2025.
